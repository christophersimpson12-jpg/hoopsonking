Chris Simpson · Program Director
Background

A St. Benedict's Prep Class of '10 alumnus, Chris Simpson has spent over 15 years building one of the most well-rounded coaching resumes in the country — from the NBA to collegiate programs to elite high school basketball.

Coach Simpson began at The Hive as a student manager under Dan Hurley — learning the discipline and competitive standard that defines Benedict's basketball. He went on to manage at St. John's under Steve Lavin before interning with the Brooklyn Nets under Head Coach Jason Kidd during the NBA Playoffs, where he assisted with draft preparation and playoff scouting.

He built his coaching career at IMG Academy in Bradenton, FL, Wagner College, Loyola Marymount University under Mike Dunlap, and Dymally High School in Los Angeles — where he coached the program to the 2020 LA City Championship and was named CIF Coach of the Year.

Most recently, Coach Simpson served as Director of Scouting & Video and then Assistant Coach at the University of San Diego under Steve Lavin. He holds a Master of Arts in School Counseling from Loyola Marymount University and a B.S. in Sports Management from St. John's University.
