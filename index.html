Timeout
Background

A St. Benedict's Prep Class of '10 alumnus, Chris Simpson has spent over 15 years building one of the most well-rounded coaching resumes in the country — from the NBA to collegiate programs to elite high school basketball.

Coach Simpson began at The Hive as a student manager under Dan Hurley. He went on to manage at St. John's under Steve Lavin, interned with the Brooklyn Nets under Jason Kidd during the NBA Playoffs, and built his coaching career at IMG Academy, Wagner College, Loyola Marymount, and Dymally High School — where he won the 2020 LA City Championship and was named CIF Coach of the Year.

Most recently, Coach Simpson served as Assistant Coach and Director of Scouting & Video at the University of San Diego under Steve Lavin. He holds a Master's in School Counseling from Loyola Marymount University.
